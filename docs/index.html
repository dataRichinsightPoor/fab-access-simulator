FcRn geometry: schematic

Weise et al., Fig. 1A, C, E and F shows the proposed endosomal FcRn orientation and the N–N versus C–C format architecture. The two Fabs move from the N-terminal side to the C-terminal side of Fc; the schematics do not supply angles, distances, or an intact golimumab structure.

Golimumab: measured outcomes

Weise et al., Fig. 8A–D compares soluble TNF neutralization, FcRn recycling, serum exposure, and clearance for the parent, C–C invertbody, and conventional ACT5 variant. These panels do not test intact-antibody binding kinetics on membrane TNF.

Membrane TNF: unresolved

5YOY lacks the full Fab constant domains, hinge, Fc, membrane TNF stalk and bilayer. Three separate Fvs in the crystal do not specify the valency or orientation of any one intact antibody. The viewer assigns no clash, allowable approach angle, accessible fraction, or kinetic rate.

05 / Inspect the model

What the simulator represents

Math and units

dB/dt = k_on C(t)(f − B) − (k_off + k_loss)B

B is the fraction of all surface sites occupied, f is the reachable fraction, and C(t) is free antibody in the tissue. Time is in hours; concentration is in nM. The solver uses the exact constant-input update with concentration evaluated at each step’s midpoint. Uploaded profiles are interpolated linearly and their knot times are included in the integration grid.

What the model cannot infer

The structures provide no calibration for access or rates. This model also does not separate uptake from other occupied-site loss or predict FcRn recycling, payload cleavage, intracellular exposure or protein degradation. Those require separate measurements.

What the data say

The Weise et al. preprint measured clearance in a mouse model and soluble TNF neutralization, not the paired tissue exposures or surface binding rates needed here. The outputs are occupancy scenarios, not efficacy or dosing predictions.
