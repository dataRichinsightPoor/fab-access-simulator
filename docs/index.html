How the geometry is calculated

The origin is the centroid of the first resolved Cα positions on the three TNF chains. At zero tilt, the direction from that origin to the TNF Cα centroid is vertical. The complex then undergoes rigid rotation in the displayed plane. The dashed domain sphere is positioned by an offset and bearing from the midpoint of the last resolved Cα on the two Fv chains; the bearing is measured from horizontal at zero tilt.

Clearance is the lowest signed height above the flat plane. For TNF and Fv, it uses deposited heavy atoms with uniform 0.17 nm padding; for the hypothetical domain it uses the sphere’s lower edge. Below zero means overlap in this model. This check does not test domain–TNF or domain–Fab collisions, hinge flexibility, bivalency, or the native TNF stalk and membrane orientation.

Coordinates: RCSB PDB 5YOY, 2.73 Å, Ono et al. The sphere is not a reconstruction of a conventional or inverted IgG. Geometry does not set the occupancy model’s accessible fraction or rates.

05 / Inspect the model

What the simulator represents

Math and units

dB/dt = k_on C(t)(f − B) − (k_off + k_loss)B

B is the fraction of all surface sites occupied, f is the reachable fraction, and C(t) is free antibody in the tissue. Time is in hours; concentration is in nM. The solver uses the exact constant-input update with concentration evaluated at each step’s midpoint. Uploaded profiles are interpolated linearly and their knot times are included in the integration grid.

Structural limits

The geometry panel checks overlap with a plane under an assumed pose. It provides no calibration for occupancy. Access and rates remain user hypotheses: 5YOY resolves an Fv with soluble TNF, not an intact inverted antibody at a membrane.

What the data say

The Weise et al. preprint measured clearance in a mouse model and soluble TNF neutralization, not the paired tissue exposures or surface binding rates needed here. The outputs are occupancy scenarios, not efficacy or dosing predictions.
